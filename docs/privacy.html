Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

iRetroMusic is published by Orch Aerospace, Inc. This policy explains how the app handles information. In short: it collects nothing.

Data collection

iRetroMusic does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs, and it makes no network requests of its own.

Music library

With your permission the app reads the song list of the Music app's library on your device (titles, artists, durations and identifiers) so you can record songs onto tapes and play them. This information stays on your device.

Stored on your device

Your tapes (names, lengths, colours and running orders) are saved in the app's own storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
