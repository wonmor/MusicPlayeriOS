Support

Questions, bugs and ideas

Email john@orchestrsim.com with your device, iOS version and what happened. We usually reply within two working days.

How do I make a tape?

Open the shelf (the stack icon at the top right on iPhone), tap New mixtape, choose the length, label and shell colour, then tap Record songs onto side A. Switch to side B to record the other side, then Save.

Why won't a song record?

Each side only holds half the cassette's running time: 23 minutes on a C46, 30 on a C60 and 45 on a C90. A song is recorded only if it fits the time left. Choose a longer tape or use side B.

What do the keys do?

REW goes back to the start of the song (twice for the previous song), F.FWD skips to the next song, PLAY plays or pauses, STOP stops, and FLIP turns the tape over. With Auto reverse on, side B plays after side A.

My songs don't appear.

iRetroMusic lists the songs in the Music app's library on your device. If you declined access, turn it on in Settings, iRetroMusic, Media & Apple Music. Songs you add in the Music app appear the next time you open the song picker.

How do I edit or delete a tape?

Touch and hold the tape on the shelf, then choose Edit tape, Duplicate or Delete. The Demo Tape can't be deleted.
