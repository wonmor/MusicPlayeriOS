Stereo cassette deck | auto reverse

iRetroMusic turns your music library into mixtapes and plays them on a hi-fi cassette deck for iPhone.

Make a mixtape

Pick a cassette length (C46, C60 or C90), write the label and choose a shell colour. Record songs onto side A and side B. Each side holds half the running time, so a song is only recorded if it fits. Reorder by dragging, or fill the rest of a side at random.

Play it on the deck

REW, PLAY, F.FWD, STOP and FLIP piano keys. The tape winds from reel to reel as the side plays, the three-digit counter turns, and auto-reverse flips to side B when side A runs out.
